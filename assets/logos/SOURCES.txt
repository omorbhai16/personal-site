Logo sources (downloaded and stored locally; nothing is loaded from the internet at runtime)

Devicon (https://devicon.dev, MIT licence) - official brand colours
  html.svg, css.svg, cpp.svg, python.svg, java.svg, typescript.svg, react.svg,
  nextjs.svg, git.svg, visual-studio.svg, vscode.svg, github.svg
  (github.svg: the official black mark recoloured white so it is visible on a dark background,
   which GitHub's logo guidelines permit)

gilbarbara/logos (https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos) - full-colour brand logos
  whatsapp.svg, facebook.svg

SuperTinyIcons (https://github.com/edent/SuperTinyIcons) - Gmail multicolour "M"
  gmail.svg (white background tile removed, viewBox cropped to the artwork)

All logos remain trademarks of their respective owners; use them only to refer to those products/services.
