About Me

I'm Omor Faruk, Founder & CEO based in Bangladesh. I work directly with clients to understand their goals, requirements and expectations, then lead the project from planning through final delivery.

I own and lead a digital company supported by a team of 25+ professionals working across different service areas. My role isn't to personally perform every task. Instead, I stay directly involved with the client, organize the project, coordinate the right people, monitor progress and personally review the final result before delivery.

For me, a project is more than a task to complete — it's a responsibility. I want every client to have a clear point of contact, a structured process, and confidence that someone is personally overseeing the work from beginning to end.

The Workflow

How I Work

I keep the client relationship direct and the project process structured. You work with me, while the team behind me handles the execution required for the project.

01

Contact Me

You contact me directly with your idea, requirement or business goal.

02

Requirement Discussion

I discuss your goals, requirements, budget, timeline and expectations with you.

03

Project Planning

I turn the requirements into a practical plan with clear priorities and milestones.

04

Team & Category Assignment

I identify the relevant service category and assign the work to the appropriate Category Leader and team members.

05

Project Execution

The assigned team members carry out the required work while the Category Leader oversees it within that service area.

06

Team Member Self-Review

The person or people who completed the work review it first and correct any issues they find.

07

Category Leader Review

The relevant Category Leader reviews the work for quality, completeness and project requirements.

08

My Final Review

After the Category Leader approves the work, it comes to me for a final review.

09

Final Delivery

Once I approve the completed work, it is delivered to you.

10

Ongoing Support

I remain available after delivery for future updates, improvements, fixes and other project-related support.
