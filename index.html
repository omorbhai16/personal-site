ABOUT ME

Introduction

I'm OMOR ALI, a Web Developer, Software Developer and AI Automation Specialist. I build modern websites, software and AI-powered automation, and I enjoy fixing problems in them too.

My Development Journey

There was a time when I didn't even know how to use a keypad phone, while friends my age were already using smartphones. I was fascinated by them – games, videos and everything inside. Whenever I got hold of someone's phone I would open every app, without knowing what each one did, and sometimes I caused problems. That curiosity grew into a dream: to build the things that live inside a phone – software, apps and games. That dream brought me to where I am today.

Experience & Background

I can build websites, AI solutions and software, and I can also troubleshoot and fix issues in them. I haven't learned game development yet, but it remains on my list. I studied in Class 9 at Nasirabad Hetalkhali Darus Sunnah Dakhil Madrasa and completed my SSC 2026 examination at Shyamnagar Alim Madrasa. Today I continue my studies alongside my work.

Goals, Mission & Vision

My goal is to keep learning and growing as a developer – and one day to build games too – while delivering reliable, useful web, software and AI automation solutions that save people time and solve real problems.
