About Me

I’m Omor, a professional logo designer with 7+ years of experience creating unique, memorable, and professional brand identities.

Over the years, I’ve completed 700+ logo design projects for businesses, startups, entrepreneurs, and personal brands. I focus on understanding what a brand stands for and turning that idea into a visual identity that is simple, meaningful, and memorable.

I started my freelance journey by working with clients through platforms like Fiverr and Upwork. Today, I primarily connect with new clients through LinkedIn and communicate with them professionally through Email.

My goal is simple — to create logos that not only look great, but also help businesses build a strong and recognizable brand.

Concept First

Every great logo starts with a strong idea. I focus on understanding the brand, its audience, and its purpose before creating the design.

Brand-focused

A logo is more than just a symbol. I design with the bigger brand identity in mind, creating visuals that work across digital and print platforms.

Built to last

Trends come and go. I create clean, timeless logo designs that remain professional, memorable, and effective for years to come.

Detail Driven

Great design is in the details. I carefully refine every shape, spacing, proportion, and visual element to create a logo that feels balanced and professional.

Always Connected

I maintain clear and regular communication with my clients throughout every project to ensure smooth collaboration and a final design they’re truly happy with.
